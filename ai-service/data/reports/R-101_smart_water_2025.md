## Executive summary

The global smart water meter market was valued at USD 4.21 billion in 2024 and is expected to grow at a CAGR of 11.2% from 2025 to 2030. Asia-Pacific accounted for 38% of 2024 revenue.
This report includes hardware, software and services. The figure is a fictional assignment datum.

## Market definition and scope
### Page 9
Smart water metering comprises connected meters, communications modules, meter data management software, installation, and recurring analytics services. The market size includes hardware, software, and services sold for utility deployment. It excludes general-purpose water treatment equipment and non-metering sensors.

## Regional analysis
### Page 10
Asia-Pacific represents 38% of global 2024 revenue in this report's broad market scope. Utility modernization and non-revenue-water programs are key demand drivers. The regional share should be applied only to this report's stated global base.

## Forecast and assumptions
### Page 11
CMR reports a 2025-2030 CAGR of 11.2%. The forecast assumes continued smart-meter replacement programs, stable component availability, and expansion of utility data platforms. No numeric forecast beyond 2030 is provided.
