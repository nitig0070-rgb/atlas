## Executive summary
### Page 3
The synthetic industrial IoT sensor market covers connected sensing hardware and embedded connectivity for industrial monitoring. The illustrative 2024 global value is USD 9.6 billion.

## Market definition
### Page 5
Included categories are pressure, temperature, vibration, flow, and environmental sensors with industrial connectivity. Standalone consumer devices and general-purpose control software are excluded.

## Regional outlook
### Page 7
Asia-Pacific and North America are the two largest fictional demand centers. The report does not publish a numeric regional share.

## Forecast and assumptions
### Page 10
The synthetic report gives a 12.4% CAGR for 2025-2030, assuming factory retrofit investment and wider predictive-maintenance adoption. It does not provide a 2035 value.

## Methodology
### Page 12
All values are illustrative, based on a fictional supplier panel and analyst estimates. They are not real-world market research.
